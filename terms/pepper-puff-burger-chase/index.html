About the game

Pepper Puff: Burger Chase is a game from Astrakin Games Studio. The intended release provides access to the main game without registering an account or buying cosmetic items. Additional features may vary by device, platform and version.

Accounts and eligibility

Players may use guest mode without signing in. The release plan requires sign-in with a supported Google or Apple account before any in-app purchase. The signed Android Internal testing bundle disables public accounts and purchases. Supported production providers remain pending. Players and their parents or guardians are responsible for keeping access to their sign-in accounts secure and for approving purchases where required by applicable law and platform rules. A parental gate is a product safeguard, not a guarantee that a user is legally an adult.

Digital purchases

When enabled, purchases are processed by the relevant platform's billing service and may be restored or revoked according to verified store records. Do not make any purchase unless the final game displays its price and terms before confirmation.
