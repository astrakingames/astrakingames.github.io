Pepper Puff: Burger Chase

Privacy policy

Draft for developer and legal review. This reflects the inspected development project, not a confirmed final release. It is not yet effective and should not be submitted to an app store.

Who we are

Astrakin Games Studio develops Pepper Puff: Burger Chase. Contact us at astrakingames@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress, settings, purchase entitlement information and gameplay-related history locally on your device. Removing app data or uninstalling the game may remove local progress; store purchases may be restored through the applicable store account. Device backup settings may affect what is retained.

Purchases

The Android project integrates Google Play Billing. Google processes payments under its own policies. The game is designed to use purchase information, including purchase tokens and product identifiers, for verification and restoration. The project includes a purchase verification service, but its production deployment and retention practices have not been confirmed. The iOS project includes a StoreKit adapter, but the native integration and release have not been verified.

Advertising

The project contains an optional Google AdMob integration for Android rewarded ads. The inspected configuration uses child-directed treatment and restricts personalization; the final release configuration, SDK behavior and whether ads will be enabled require confirmation. Google may process information such as network and device information when its services are used. Removed advertising-ID permissions do not establish that no device information is processed. iOS advertising is not enabled in the inspected configuration.

Analytics and diagnostics

The project includes local development analytics that can record gameplay events and a temporary session identifier on test devices. The inspected release configuration does not automatically enable the built-in development providers; no remote analytics provider or crash-reporting SDK was identified in the project review. Final build dependencies and settings must be checked before release.

Children and families

The project includes features intended for a child audience, including a parental gate. Parents and guardians can contact us to ask about or request deletion of a child’s information. The final audience, consent practices and third-party settings must be reviewed before release.

Sharing, retention and deletion

Store providers process purchases, and Google may process data if AdMob is enabled. If purchase verification is deployed, the verification provider and its hosting services may process transaction information and network data. Local game data remains on the device until removed, subject to device backups. Support emails are handled through email services. Actual server and support retention periods, deletion procedures and any additional recipients require confirmation.

To make a privacy request, email astrakingames@gmail.com. Please do not include passwords or payment card details.

Effective date: To be set when the final policy is approved and published.
