We're here to help

Player support

Contact us at astrakingames@gmail.com for player support, purchase assistance, privacy inquiries, and parent or guardian requests.

General support

When reporting a problem, include the game name, your device model, operating system version, and a description of what happened. Please do not share passwords or payment card details.

Purchases and restoration

For purchase issues, keep your store receipt and check that you are signed in to the store account used for the purchase. Purchase and refund requests may also be handled through Google Play or the App Store.

Account deletion

For the proposed email process to request deletion of your Pepper Puff: Burger Chase account and associated data without reinstalling the game, see our Delete Account & Data page. The studio is still verifying how these requests will be processed.

Privacy and family inquiries

Players and parents or guardians can email us with privacy questions or requests regarding a child’s information. Please avoid including sensitive information in your initial message.
