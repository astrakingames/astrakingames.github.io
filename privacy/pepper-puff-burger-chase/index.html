Pepper Puff: Burger Chase

Privacy Policy

Pre-release draft. This page needs final engineering verification and legal approval before it can be used for a store submission. The proposed release features are not yet confirmed as live.

Last reviewed: 12 November 2026. Effective date: Not yet assigned. This is a draft describing the intended Pepper Puff: Burger Chase release; it must be reconciled to the shipped builds and backend before becoming effective.

Who we are and how to contact us

Astrakin Games Studio develops Pepper Puff: Burger Chase. For privacy questions, parental requests, or concerns about personal information, contact astrakingames@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, payment-card information or full purchase tokens by email.

Guest play and information on your device

The game is designed to allow all core gameplay without registration. Local device data may include unlocked levels, progress, settings, gameplay history, and saved preferences. Removing the app or its data may remove local progress; platform backup behavior can vary by device and release settings.

Optional Google or Apple sign-in

The intended release requires Google or Apple sign-in before a player can make an in-app purchase; sign-in is not required to play. The signed Android Internal testing build supports guest play and disables public accounts and purchases. The QA backend stores the provider name and account identifier and supports encrypted Apple refresh credentials. Its account records have no email or display-name field. The exact fields and platforms supported must be confirmed from the production authentication configuration. Account creation and linking must be disabled if the associated deletion flow is not operational.

In-app purchases and entitlements

Google Play or Apple, as applicable, processes payments under its own policies; Astrakin Games does not need a player's payment-card number to provide cosmetic purchases. If purchases are enabled, the game and its backend may receive product identifiers, store purchase tokens or transaction identifiers, verification state, and related entitlement records needed to prevent fraud, unlock and restore purchases and handle refunds or revocations. The final server hosting, logging practices and retention schedule need confirmation.

Advertisements

The signed Android Internal testing build disables ads and excludes AdMob. Rewarded and interstitial ads are planned features. If ads are enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information including IP address, device or advertising identifiers where available, approximate location derived from network information, app interactions and diagnostic data. Actual data collection depends on the shipping SDK, consent signals, audience settings and implementation. Review Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure guidance and Privacy Policy. We must verify the final children's-audience configuration and any advertising partners before publishing this policy.

Backend, cloud saves and diagnostics

The QA backend uses ASP.NET Core and PostgreSQL for accounts and purchase verification and supports cloud saves. Guest progress stays on the device. Production deployment and cloud save availability remain pending. If deployed, our hosting provider may process service logs, IP address, timestamps and security events needed to operate and protect the service. Cloud save information, if collected, may include level progress and player preferences. The final deployment, whether cloud saves are enabled, analytics/crash-reporting SDKs, data recipients and retention periods must be checked before release.

How information is used and shared

Subject to the final implementation, information may be processed for account authentication, restoring cosmetics, validating store purchases, saving progress, detecting abuse, delivering requested support and displaying ads. Relevant providers may include Google for sign-in, Play purchases and ads; Apple for sign-in and App Store purchases on supported platforms; and the selected API, database and email service providers. We will not describe a provider as active unless the final build and deployment confirm its use. Any provider-specific data practices are also governed by that provider's policies.

Children and parental requests

The game is designed for families. The shipping build needs a review of the Play Console audience declaration, Families requirements and child-directed SDK settings. Guest play must remain available without an account or purchase. Parents and guardians may contact astrakingames@gmail.com about a child's account or data. Do not submit unnecessary personal information for a child.

Security and retention

Server endpoints are intended to use HTTPS/TLS and access controls, but every user-data transmission and SDK configuration must be verified before a security claim is published. Local game data remains on the device until removed or overwritten, subject to backup settings. QA deletion removes linked identities, sessions, handoff codes, cloud saves and account entitlements. The QA database keeps the deleted player record and deletion completion record. It also keeps purchase verification records with the account reference cleared. Actual production retention periods and legal grounds for retention require confirmation. If some records must be retained for lawful security, fraud-prevention or accounting reasons, their categories and periods must be disclosed in the final policy.

Your privacy choices and deletion

For the proposed email request process, visit our account and data deletion page or contact astrakingames@gmail.com. The shipping app must also have a clear in-app path to initiate deletion. Deleting a Pepper Puff account does not delete the separate Google or Apple identity account, and store purchase history may remain with the platform. The precise handling of retained verification or accounting records must be verified and disclosed before launch.

Updates

Changes to this notice will be reflected on this page with a new effective date when approved. See our Terms of Service or Support for related information.
