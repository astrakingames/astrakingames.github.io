Pepper Puff: Burger Chase

Delete Account & Data

Pre-release draft. This page needs final engineering verification and legal approval before it can be used for a store submission. The proposed release features are not yet confirmed as live.

For: Pepper Puff: Burger Chase by Astrakin Games Studio.

Request account and associated data deletion

The proposed email process allows requests without reinstalling or opening the game. The button opens a prefilled email draft. Use the email link below to contact our support team. In your message, say that you want to delete your Pepper Puff: Burger Chase account and its associated data. The studio is still verifying the process for confirming account ownership.

Request account deletion by email

If the email button does not work, send a message directly to astrakingames@gmail.com with the subject Pepper Puff — Delete Account Request.

What information your request covers

In the QA backend, deletion removes provider identities, sessions, handoff codes, cloud progress and account entitlements. It keeps the deleted player ID and timestamps, a linked deletion completion record and purchase verification records with the account reference cleared. Local game progress and platform purchase history are handled separately. Transaction or security records may require limited retention for lawful accounting, fraud prevention or compliance reasons; the final categories and periods must be published in the production privacy policy. An account deletion request is not a request to delete your separate Google or Apple identity account.

What happens next

  1. We receive your request at our support email.
  2. We may ask for limited information to verify that you control the relevant game account. Never email passwords, payment card information or purchase tokens.
  3. After verification, the production backend and support process must delete the relevant account and associated data, with any lawful retention explained to you.
  4. We will respond about completion or any information required to process the request.

Before this page is used for Play Console compliance, the team must verify an operational mailbox, real deletion capability, staff ownership and a documented response timeline. Account freezing alone is not deletion.

In-app deletion

QA configurations that expose account tools provide Account & Cloud Saves → Account Settings → Delete Account. The flow requires recent sign-in and confirmation. Public accounts are disabled in the signed Android Internal testing bundle. The in-app path and deletion outcome still need testing on the build that will offer accounts.

Delete data without deleting your account

For questions about deleting a specific category of game data, email astrakingames@gmail.com describing your request. Availability of partial deletion must be confirmed against the backend before it is represented as an app feature.
